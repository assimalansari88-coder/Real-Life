# المزارع الفقير – الحلقة 1: «سر سعيد»
### The Poor Farmer – Episode 1: "Saeed's Secret"

**طريقة الاستخدام:**
1. انسخ «وصف الشخصيات» والصقه **في بداية كل أمر (prompt)**. هذا يخلي شكل الشخصيات ثابت من مشهد لمشهد.
2. لكل مشهد عندك:
   - **Image prompt:** يولّد صورة المشهد.
   - **Motion prompt:** يحرّك الصورة ويحوّلها لفيديو (image-to-video).
3. الأدوات المناسبة: Kling، Runway، Hailuo، Sora، Veo، Pika. أغلبها يعطي رصيد مجاني يومي.
4. اختر المقاس **9:16** وطول **5 ثواني** لكل مشهد، يعني الحلقة كاملة 50 ثانية تقريباً.
5. ركّب المشاهد بالترتيب في CapCut، وأضف الموسيقى المقترحة تحت.

## وصف الشخصيات (Character bible — انسخه مع كل مشهد)

```
Style: photorealistic cinematic film still, vertical 9:16, anamorphic lens look, shallow depth of field, film grain, rich color grading, Gulf/Bahraini village setting with palm trees, sand-colored stone houses and wooden market stalls.

SAEED: Bahraini man, 35, very thin, short black beard, tired sad brown eyes, sun-darkened skin, wearing an old faded brown thobe with torn sleeves and patched hem, simple white cap (taqiyah), barefoot sandals. Humble posture.

THE GUARDIAN (Saeed at night): tall figure in a flowing black abaya-like cloak with a long cape, hood up, carved dark wooden mask covering the whole face with two eye holes that glow bright emerald green, faint green light and dust swirling around his hands.

MARYAM: kind young Arab woman, early 20s, black abaya and black headscarf, soft warm face, gentle smile.

SHEIKH RASHID: old rich Arab man, 65, grey beard, spotless white thobe, white ghutra with black agal, gold watch and prayer beads, arrogant expression.

NASSER: Arab young man, 22, clean-shaven with thin mustache, white thobe with sleeves rolled, cocky grin.
```

## المشهد 1 – شروق الشمس في المزرعة (5 ث)
**Image prompt:**
```
Sunrise over a small green farm on the edge of a Bahraini village. SAEED works alone, watering rows of leafy vegetables with an old metal watering can. Golden low sun behind palm trees, mist over the field. His face is tired and sad. Wide shot, he is small in the frame, lonely mood.
```
**Motion prompt:** `Slow push-in toward Saeed as water pours from the can, he wipes sweat from his forehead and sighs. Gentle mist drifting, warm sun flare.`
**Sound:** أصوات عصافير، ماء، عود حزين وهادئ.

## المشهد 2 – الساحة: رمي الطماطم (5 ث)
**Image prompt:**
```
Sunny village square with stone houses. NASSER and three friends laugh loudly and throw tomatoes at SAEED. Tomato juice splashes on Saeed's brown thobe. Saeed stands still holding a small basket. Medium shot, harsh midday sunlight.
```
**Motion prompt:** `A tomato flies and splashes on Saeed's shoulder, the young men laugh and point. Saeed slowly wipes his face with his sleeve and gives a small sad smile. Handheld camera.`
**Sound:** ضحك ساخر، صوت ارتطام الطماطم.

## المشهد 3 – السوق: الشيخ راشد يصرخ (5 ث)
**Image prompt:**
```
Busy traditional Bahraini souq with spice sacks and fabric canopies. SHEIKH RASHID points his finger and shouts angrily at SAEED. Villagers watch and whisper in the background. Saeed looks down humbly, hands together. Low-angle shot on Rashid to make him dominant.
```
**Motion prompt:** `Sheikh Rashid shouts and waves his prayer beads, Saeed lowers his head further. Slow camera dolly around them, background crowd murmuring.`
**Sound:** ضوضاء السوق، صوت غاضب، صمت لحظة عند نظرة سعيد للأرض.

## المشهد 4 – السوق: مريم والخبز (5 ث)
**Image prompt:**
```
Quiet corner of the souq in warm golden afternoon light. MARYAM secretly hands SAEED a round piece of fresh Arabic bread wrapped in cloth, glancing around so nobody sees. She smiles kindly, he smiles shyly. Close two-shot, soft bokeh background.
```
**Motion prompt:** `Maryam slides the bread into Saeed's hands, their eyes meet briefly, he gives a shy grateful smile and she walks away. Soft slow motion.`
**Sound:** موسيقى دافئة خفيفة (بيانو أو قانون).

## المشهد 5 – ليلة العاصفة: هجوم اللصوص (5 ث)
**Image prompt:**
```
Dark stormy night in the village. Masked thieves in black clothes with face scarves attack, a market stall on fire, heavy rain, lightning in the sky. Villagers run and scream in panic. Orange fire light mixed with blue storm light. Wide chaotic shot.
```
**Motion prompt:** `Lightning flashes, fire flickers in the rain, thieves run through the street knocking over baskets, villagers flee toward the camera. Shaky handheld camera.`
**Sound:** رعد، مطر، صراخ، طبول توتر.

## المشهد 6 – المزرعة ليلاً: ظهور القوة (5 ث)
**Image prompt:**
```
Night, rain on the farm. SAEED kneels alone in the mud and presses his palm on the ground. The earth cracks around his hand with glowing green light in the cracks. His eyes glow bright green. Giant vines and plants burst from the soil growing into a huge green wall behind him. Low-angle dramatic shot.
```
**Motion prompt:** `Saeed's hand touches the ground, cracks spread outward glowing green, his eyes light up, vines erupt and rise quickly into a towering wall. Camera tilts up following the growth.`
**Sound:** هدير أرض، نبضة صوت عميقة، موسيقى ملحمية تبدأ.

## المشهد 7 – الحارس يمشي في العاصفة (5 ث)
**Image prompt:**
```
THE GUARDIAN walks slowly down the middle of a dark flooded village street in the storm, cape flying in the strong wind, glowing green eyes behind the wooden mask, burning stall far in the background. Symmetrical heroic composition, rain streaks, rim light from lightning.
```
**Motion prompt:** `The Guardian walks toward the camera in slow motion, cape whipping in the wind, rain falling, lightning flash reveals the mask. Slow camera pull-back.`
**Sound:** ريح قوية، خطوات ثقيلة، موسيقى بطولية.

## المشهد 8 – القتال (5 ث)
**Image prompt:**
```
THE GUARDIAN fights five masked thieves in the muddy street. Walls of earth burst up from the ground between them, dust and wind swirling, the Guardian a dark blur of super speed with green light trails. Thieves thrown back and running away. Dynamic action shot.
```
**Motion prompt:** `The Guardian dashes between thieves leaving green light trails, slams his palm down and earth walls shoot up, thieves stumble and run away into the dark. Fast camera whip-pans.`
**Sound:** ضربات، اندفاع أرض، صيحات اللصوص وهم يهربون.

## المشهد 9 – إنقاذ ناصر (5 ث)
**Image prompt:**
```
A burning wooden beam falls toward NASSER who is frozen in fear. THE GUARDIAN grabs Nasser's arm and pulls him to safety just in time, sparks and debris crashing behind them. Nasser's face is shocked, staring at the glowing green eyes. Close dramatic shot.
```
**Motion prompt:** `The beam falls, the Guardian yanks Nasser aside, debris crashes where he stood. Nasser looks up in shock — the Guardian steps back into the shadows and vanishes in a swirl of dust.`
**Sound:** تكسّر خشب، صمت مفاجئ، همسة ريح عند الاختفاء.

## المشهد 10 – الصباح: البطل المجهول (5 ث)
**Image prompt:**
```
Bright peaceful morning after the storm. Villagers celebrate in the square, clapping and cheering about the unknown hero, children drawing a green-eyed mask on a wall. In the foreground SAEED walks alone in his torn brown thobe, ignored by everyone, with a small secret smile. Warm hopeful light.
```
**Motion prompt:** `Saeed walks slowly past the cheering crowd, nobody looks at him, he glances at the drawing of the mask and smiles quietly. Camera tracks beside him, then rises to a wide shot of the village.`
**Sound:** هتاف وفرح، ثم تهدأ الموسيقى، ونص على الشاشة: «يتبع...».
